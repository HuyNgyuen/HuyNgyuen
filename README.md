## 📄 Research

**Improving Deep Learning Robustness Against Adversarial Image Attacks**<br/>
*Lê Đình Hiển, Nguyễn Huỳnh Minh Học, **Nguyễn Đỗ Huy**, Nguyễn Gia Huy, Lý Ngọc Quân, Hà Thanh Dũng*<br/>
National Conference on Artificial Intelligence (FJCAI), Cần Thơ, 2026

We benchmarked FGSM, BIM and PGD attacks on MobileNetV2 and compared three defenses: a denoising autoencoder, input randomization and adversarial training. Against strong attacks, average accuracy rose from **4.0%** without defense to **45.0%** with the denoising autoencoder.
